# Code of Conduct

This is a place to learn. Everyone is welcome, at any level.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient. Questions that look simple are welcome.
- Criticise the note or the code, never the person.
- Assume good intent. Many people here write in their second language.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, or personal attacks.
- Hateful or sexual content of any kind.
- Spam, advertising, or links to pirated books and courses.
- Sharing someone's private information.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close issues, or block users who break
these rules. To report a problem, open an issue or contact the maintainer through GitHub.

Based on the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
